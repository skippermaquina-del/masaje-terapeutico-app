# Effleurage

## Definition
- Light, gliding stroke
- Opens & closes a session
- Links other strokes

## Technique
- Palm / fingers / forearm
- Direction: centripetal (toward heart)
- Pressure: superficial <-> deep
- Constant skin contact

## Physiological Effects
- Blood & lymph circulation
- Warms tissue
- Sensory nerve stimulation -> relaxation
- Assessment tool (tone, temp, texture)

## Indications
- Warm-up / closing
- General relaxation
- Lymphatic drainage (light pressure)

## Contraindications
- Acute inflammation / infection
- DVT / suspected clot
- Open wounds / burns
- Undiagnosed lumps
