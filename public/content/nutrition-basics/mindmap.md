# Nutrition Basics

## Scope
- Basic info only
- No diet plans, no supplement dosing
- Refer to dietitian/physician

## Six Nutrients
- Water: ~half body weight in oz
- Protein: 12-20%, repairs (doesn't build) muscle
- Carbohydrates: 55-60%, primary fuel
  - Simple vs Complex
  - High vs Low glycemic
- Fats: <30%
  - Saturated (bad, solid)
  - Unsaturated (better, liquid)
  - HDL (good) vs LDL (bad)
- Vitamins: no calories
  - Water-soluble (B, C): excreted
  - Fat-soluble (A, D, E, K): stored, toxicity risk
- Minerals: trace amounts
  - Iron, Calcium

## Memory Aids
- Protein/carb = 4 cal/g
- Fat = 9 cal/g
- HDL = Happy, LDL = Lousy

## Herbs/Supplements
- Treat like medicine
- No anecdotal dosing
- Outside scope to advise directly
