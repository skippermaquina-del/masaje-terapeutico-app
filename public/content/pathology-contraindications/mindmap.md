# Pathology & Contraindications

## Basic Vocabulary
- Pathology / Etiology
- Sign (objective) vs Symptom (subjective)
- Acute vs Chronic
- Inflammation
- Infection
- Edema
- Autoimmune disease

## Local vs Systemic
- Local: avoid the area only
- Systemic: avoid the whole session

## Absolute Contraindications
- DVT / suspected clot
- Fever
- Sepsis / active systemic infection
- Active TB
- Recent MI or stroke
- Unhealed fractures

## Condition Categories
- Infection risk -> contraindicated (MRSA, cellulitis, active hepatitis, fever)
- Clot risk -> contraindicated (DVT)
- Fragile tissue -> gentle pressure, not avoidance (osteoporosis)
- Neurological -> indicated with caution (MS, Parkinson's)
- Autoimmune flare -> local avoidance during flare (RA)
- Chronic pain -> generally indicated (fibromyalgia, OA)

## Exam Strategy
- Ask: local or systemic?
- Ask: infection, clot, or fragile tissue involved?
