# Body Movements & Joint Actions

## Sagittal Plane
- Flexion / Extension / Hyperextension
- Dorsiflexion / Plantarflexion

## Frontal Plane
- Abduction / Adduction
- Elevation / Depression
- Lateral flexion

## Transverse Plane
- Medial / Lateral rotation
- Pronation / Supination
- Inversion / Eversion

## Special Movements
- Circumduction
- Protraction / Retraction
- Scapular upward/downward rotation

## By Region
- Shoulder: most movements (ball & socket)
- Elbow: flexion/extension only (hinge)
- Forearm: pronation/supination
- Ankle: dorsi/plantarflexion, inversion/eversion
- Spine: flexion/extension/lateral flexion/rotation
