# Muscles of Head & Neck

## Facial Expression / Mastication
- Epicranius: raises eyebrow
- Orbicularis oculi: closes eye
- Orbicularis oris: closes/purses lips
- Buccinator: compresses cheek
- Zygomaticus: raises mouth corner (smile)
- Masseter: elevates mandible (closes jaw)
- Temporalis: closes jaw
- Platysma: depresses mandible, tenses neck skin

## Neck Movers
- SCM: unilateral = lateral flex + rotate to OPPOSITE side; bilateral = flexes neck
- Splenius capitis: unilateral = rotate/flex same side; bilateral = extends head
- Splenius cervicis: similar, lower attachment
- Scalenes: lateral flexion; elevate ribs 1-2 in forced inhalation

## Mastication Muscles
- Masseter + Temporalis (close jaw)
- Medial/lateral pterygoid (grinding, opening)

## Study Strategy
- Action first
- Then origin/insertion
- Group by shared action
