# Muscles of Hip & Thigh

## Hip Flexors
- Psoas major + Iliacus = Iliopsoas
- Insert at lesser trochanter

## Gluteals
- Gluteus maximus: extends, laterally rotates, abducts hip
- Gluteus medius/minimus: abduct, medially rotate (pelvic stability)
- TFL: abducts, flexes, medially rotates

## Deep Six Lateral Rotators
- Piriformis, quadratus femoris, obturator int/ext, gemellus sup/inf
- All laterally rotate hip
- Sciatic nerve runs near/through piriformis -> piriformis syndrome

## Adductors
- Adductor longus/magnus/brevis, pectineus, gracilis
- Adduct + flex hip (mostly)
- Gracilis crosses knee too

## Anterior/Lateral Thigh
- Sartorius: hip flex/abduct/lat rotate + knee flex (2 joints, longest muscle)
- Quadriceps: rectus femoris (crosses hip+knee), vastus x3 (knee only)

## Pes Anserine
- Sartorius + Gracilis + Semitendinosus

## Hamstrings (posterior)
- Biceps femoris, semitendinosus, semimembranosus
- Flex knee, extend hip

## Pelvic Tilt
- Anterior: tight hip flexors, weak hip extensors
- Posterior: reverse pattern
