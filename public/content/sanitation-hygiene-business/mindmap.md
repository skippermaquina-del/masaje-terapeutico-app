# Sanitation, Hygiene & Business

## Sanitation Vocabulary
- Handwashing (most effective measure)
- Disinfection
- Universal precautions
- Cross-contamination prevention
- Laundering protocol (per client, per session)
- Gloves for broken skin/fluids

## Table & Equipment
- Clean linens every session
- Disinfect table/face cradle/tools
- Personal hygiene

## Hydrotherapy
- Heat -> vasodilation -> chronic tissue
- Cold -> vasoconstriction -> acute injury
- Rule: acute inflammation = cold, not heat
- Heat effects: increased blood flow, pulse, dilation (NOT decreased metabolism)

## Business & Professional Practice
- Documentation as legal safeguard
- Ethical advertising (no false cure claims)
- Record retention per regulation
- Risk management (insurance, consent)
- Therapist self-care (body mechanics, career longevity)

## Exam Scenarios
- Skin lesions -> avoid, follow protocol
- Reused unwashed linen -> sanitation violation
- Acute swollen injury -> cold, not heat
