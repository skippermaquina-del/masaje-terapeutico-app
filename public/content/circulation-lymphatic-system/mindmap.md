# Circulation & Lymphatic System

## Cardiovascular Vocabulary
- Artery: away from heart
- Vein: toward heart, has valves
- Capillary: exchange site
- Oxygenated vs deoxygenated blood

## Circulation Loops
- Pulmonary: heart <-> lungs
- Systemic: heart <-> body

## Lymphatic System
- Lymph: fluid from plasma, carries waste
- Lymphatic vessels: toward heart, no central pump
- Lymph nodes: filter, immune cells
- Edema vs Lymphedema

## Massage Direction
- Centripetal strokes assist venous/lymphatic return
- Light pressure for lymphatic work
- Firmer pressure OK for general venous assist

## Clinical Contraindications
- DVT: absolute contraindication (embolism risk)
- Varicosities: avoid deep work
- Uncontrolled hypertension: caution with vigorous techniques
