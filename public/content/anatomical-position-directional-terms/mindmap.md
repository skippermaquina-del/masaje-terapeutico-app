# Anatomical Position & Directional Terms

## Anatomical Position
- Standing, feet flat, forward
- Arms at sides, palms forward
- Reference posture for all terms

## Forearm Positions
- Supinated: palms up, radius/ulna parallel
- Pronated: palms down, radius/ulna crossed

## Three Planes
- Sagittal: L/R split -> flexion/extension
- Frontal (coronal): front/back split -> abduction/adduction
- Transverse: top/bottom split -> rotation

## Body Cavities
- Ventral: thoracic + abdominopelvic
- Dorsal: cranial + spinal canal

## Directional Pairs
- Anterior <-> Posterior
- Superior <-> Inferior
- Medial <-> Lateral
- Proximal <-> Distal
- Superficial <-> Deep
- Ipsilateral <-> Contralateral

## Region Terms
- Palmar (hand) / Plantar (foot)
- Flexor surface / Extensor surface
