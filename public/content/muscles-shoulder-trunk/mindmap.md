# Muscles of Shoulder Girdle & Trunk

## Shoulder Girdle (move scapula/clavicle)
- Trapezius (upper/middle/lower): elevate/retract/depress scapula
- Rhomboids: retract, downward rotate
- Levator scapulae: elevate, downward rotate
- Serratus anterior: protract, upward rotate (prevents winging)
- Pectoralis minor: depress, protract, downward rotate

## Trunk / Core
- Rectus abdominis: flexes spine (six-pack)
- External/internal oblique: lateral flex + rotate / bilateral flex
- Transverse abdominis: compresses abdomen (stabilizer)
- Erector spinae: extends spine (bilateral)
- Quadratus lumborum: "hip hiker", extends lumbar spine bilaterally

## Shoulder Joint (move humerus)
- Pectoralis major: adduct, medial rotate, flex
- Latissimus dorsi: extend, adduct, medial rotate
- Deltoid: ant=flex/med rotate, mid=abduct, post=extend/lat rotate
- Teres major: extend, adduct, medial rotate
- Coracobrachialis: flex, adduct

## Rotator Cuff (SITS)
- Supraspinatus: abducts
- Infraspinatus: laterally rotates
- Teres minor: laterally rotates
- Subscapularis: medially rotates (only one, inserts lesser tubercle)
