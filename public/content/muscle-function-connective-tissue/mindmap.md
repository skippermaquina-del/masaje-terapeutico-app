# Muscle Function & Connective Tissue

## Origin vs Insertion
- Origin: fixed end
- Insertion: moving end

## Muscle Roles
- Agonist (prime mover)
- Antagonist (opposes)
- Synergist (assists, same direction)
- Fixator/Stabilizer (prevents motion)

## Contraction Types
- Isometric: tension, no movement
- Isotonic
  - Concentric: shortens
  - Eccentric: lengthens under tension

## Proprioception
- Muscle spindles -> stretch reflex (overstretch protection)
- Golgi tendon organs -> relax on high tension (overcontraction protection)

## Connective Tissues
- Tendon: muscle -> bone
- Ligament: bone -> bone (slow healing, poor blood supply)
- Fascia: superficial vs deep
- Cartilage: cushions bones
- Bursa: fluid sac, reduces friction
