# Nervous System & Dermatomes

## Major Divisions
- CNS: brain + spinal cord
- PNS: nerves outside CNS

## PNS Subsystems
- Somatic: voluntary
- Autonomic: involuntary
  - Sympathetic: fight or flight
  - Parasympathetic: rest and digest
  - Massage: slow/rhythmic -> parasympathetic; fast/stimulating -> sympathetic

## Neurons
- Sensory (afferent): body -> CNS
- Motor (efferent): CNS -> body
- Reflex: automatic, no conscious processing

## Touch Receptors
- Mechanoreceptors: pressure/vibration
- Nociceptors: pain
- Proprioceptors: body position

## Dermatomes
- Band of skin -> one spinal nerve root
- Pattern = possible nerve root issue
- Clinical red flag -> consider referral
