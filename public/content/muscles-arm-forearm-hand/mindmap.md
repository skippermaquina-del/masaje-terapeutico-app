# Muscles of Arm, Forearm & Hand

## Elbow Movers
- Biceps brachii: flexes elbow, supinates, flexes shoulder (2 joints)
- Brachialis: pure elbow flexor
- Brachioradialis: flexes elbow (neutral position)
- Triceps brachii: extends elbow (long head also extends shoulder)

## Forearm Rotators
- Supinator: supinates
- Pronator teres: pronates + assists elbow flexion
- Pronator quadratus: fine pronation
- Biceps = strongest supinator overall

## Wrist/Finger Movers
- Flexors: origin = medial epicondyle
  - Flexor carpi radialis/ulnaris, palmaris longus, flexor digitorum profundus
- Extensors: origin = lateral epicondyle
  - Extensor carpi radialis/ulnaris, extensor digitorum
- Golfer's elbow = medial; Tennis elbow = lateral

## Thumb Muscles
- Flexor/Extensor pollicis longus/brevis
- Opponens pollicis: opposition

## Clinical Relevance
- Repetitive strain injuries trace to flexor/extensor groups
