# Massage Techniques

## Petrissage
- Kneading, lifting, squeezing
- Increases circulation, reduces tension
- Avoid: varicosities, acute injury, bleeding disorders

## Friction
- Circular / cross-fiber
- Breaks adhesions, realigns collagen
- Avoid: acute inflammation, bursitis

## Tapotement
- Rhythmic tapping
- Stimulates, energizes
- Avoid: kidneys, bony prominences, clotting disorders

## Vibration
- Fine rapid shaking
- Sedates, reduces pain
- Avoid: unstable joints, severe neuropathy

## Compression
- Rhythmic pressing, no glide
- Warms muscle, reduces hypertonicity
- Avoid: DVT, severe edema, fragile skin

## Rocking
- Rhythmic mobilization
- Decreases guarding
- Avoid: vertigo, recent surgery

## Skin Rolling
- Lifts fascia
- Avoid: fragile skin, recent scars

## Myofascial Spreading
- Lengthens fascia
- Avoid: acute inflammation, recent surgery

## Deep Tissue Stripping
- Long deep glide
- Avoid: acute injury, uncontrolled HTN, clot risk

## Static/Ischemic Pressure
- Sustained point pressure -> stretch
- Avoid: vascular compromise, clotting disorders

## Joint Mobilization
- Passive ROM
- Avoid: recent surgery, arthritis flare

## Stretching
- Passive or active-assisted
- Avoid: acute strain, joint instability

## Common Red Flags
- Acute inflammation/injury
- Clot risk (DVT)
- Fragile bone/skin
- Uncontrolled cardiovascular conditions
